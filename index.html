A Letter For You
Happy Birthday, My Love

My love,

I think it's funny how everything started. Around November, I first found myself thinking about you. At the time, I didn't really know what those feelings would become, or how important you would eventually be to me.

Then January came, and I tried to find a way to get closer to you. I even had my wingman with me, although somehow, he ended up talking to Annika instead. Looking back, it's actually funny to think about. I thought maybe I had already missed my chance.

But somehow, I still found your Instagram. I followed you, and eventually, you posted a story for your close friends. Somehow, I was there. And that was the little opportunity I needed to finally make the first move.

Since then, I've fallen for so many things about you.

I love your smile. I love the little faces you make when you get ragebaited. I especially love you when you're hungry—so cute, but honestly, sometimes a little scary HAHAHA.

I love how you're always ride-or-die on me in everything. I love the way you enjoy the things you love, like sumo niku, and all the little things that make you uniquely you.

But more than anything, I love making you happy.

There's something about seeing you laugh at my stupid jokes that makes me happier than I can explain. Even when the joke is completely ridiculous, seeing that smile on your face makes it worth it every single time.

I want you to know that I'll always try my best for you. I want to give you the kind of love that makes you feel safe, appreciated, and genuinely happy. I may not always get everything right, but I promise that I'll keep learning, keep trying, and keep choosing you.

And one day, we'll finally have our PCX.

We'll ride around together with nowhere important to be. We'll look for good food, visit the places we've always talked about, discover new ones along the way, and make the kind of memories we'll still talk about years from now.

I hope you never get tired of me—my stupid jokes, my random moments, or all the little things that make us who we are.

I know that things aren't easy with the situation I'm in right now. I know there are things I can't give you yet, and sometimes I wish I could change, but I will not give up.

I'm still working. I'm still trying. I'm still doing everything I can because I believe in the future we always talk about.

Maybe I can't give you everything right now, but I can give you my promise that I'm not done building toward that future.

There are still so many places I want to go with you. So many foods I want to try with you. So many random nights, long rides, stupid conversations, and unforgettable moments that I want us to experience together.

I want to grow with you.

I want to experience life with you.

And when we eventually look back at everything—the waiting, the difficult moments, the uncertainty, and all the times we wondered what would happen next—I hope we'll be able to smile and say that it was all worth it.

Because through everything, I want it to be you.

Thank you for coming into my life. Thank you for making me laugh, for letting me love you, and for giving me something beautiful to look forward to.

I love you more than I probably know how to put into words.

And no matter how long it takes, I'll keep choosing you.

Always.

Always yours, Arvy Karlson
